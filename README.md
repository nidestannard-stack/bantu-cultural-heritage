# bantu-cultural-heritage
A personal project exploring Bantu history, African civilizations, cultural heritage, and precolonial achievements.

## Research Areas

### Precolonial African Civilizations
Exploring African societies, political systems, architecture, and technological achievements before European colonization.

### Bantu Languages and Cultural Traditions
Documenting linguistic diversity, oral histories, traditional knowledge, and cultural practices.

### African Architecture and Innovation
Examining historical sites such as Great Zimbabwe and the engineering knowledge of African societies.

### Cultural Heritage Preservation
Exploring how museums, communities, and researchers can preserve and share African heritage responsibly.
